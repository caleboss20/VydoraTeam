# ACT 1 — Hook & problem (0:00–1:30)

## SLIDE 1 — Title

**VYDORA**  
*CapCut for teams.*

Mobile-first collaborative video editor with AI assist.

**Say:**

**Show:** App splash / dashboard hero.

---

## SLIDE 2 — The industry problem

Short-form video is the default language of brands, campuses, and creators — but the toolchain is broken:

| What people need | What they use today | What breaks |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Fast AI edits on phone | CapCut, VN, InShot | Solo-first; weak live team editing |
| Team review & approval | Frame.io, Filestage | Desktop, expensive, not an editor |
| Live co-edit | Almost nowhere on mobile | Handoffs via WhatsApp / Drive |
| Client / lecturer feedback | Voice notes & “change 0:42” | No frame pin, no versions, no lock |

**Say:**  
“Editing became a team sport. The tools didn’t.”

---

## SLIDE 3 — Pain story (relatable)

1. Shooter sends clips on WhatsApp.  
2. Editor cuts in CapCut alone.  
3. Captioner works on a *copy*.  
4. Lecturer / client says “fix the part at 0:42.”  
5. Three versions fight in chat. Nobody knows which is final.

**Cost:** Time, confusion, missed deadlines, inaccessible videos (no captions), lost creative energy.

**Insight:** The next winning editor isn’t “more filters.” It’s **shared editing + AI speed + mobile-native workflow**.

---

## SLIDE 4 — Who feels this pain

1. **Creator pods** (2–10 people shipping Reels/TikTok weekly)  
2. **Students & campus clubs** (group projects, events, summit intros)  
3. **Small agencies / freelancers** (client review without enterprise Frame.io)

**Say:**  
“We’re not trying to beat CapCut at every effect. We’re winning where CapCut is weak — *teams*.”

## SLIDE 7 — Feature deep dive: Editing

**Core**
- Multi-clip timeline: split, duplicate, delete, reorder / Assemble  
- Preview + fullscreen  
- Beginner toolbar + searchable “More tools”  
- Filters, effects, color grade, crop, canvas, stabilize, auto-reframe  
- Text, title cards (Sheet), flyer, stickers, overlays / PiP + masks  
- Speed curves, transitions, keyframes, multi-cam, rough cut  
- Music, SFX, mixer (duck / denoise / EQ), voiceover, beats  
- Presets: TikTok · Reels · Shorts · square · YouTube  

**Why it matters industrially:** Creators won’t switch for collab alone — the editor must feel *good enough to ship*.

---

## SLIDE 8 — Feature deep dive: AI suite

| AI tool | Real job it replaces |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Auto captions** | Manual typing; accessibility for campus / brands |
| **Silence / filler detect** | Tedious rough cuts of ums & dead air |
| **Shorts suggest** | “Where’s the hook?” hunting |
| **Beat detect** | Manual music sync |
| **Auto-reframe** | Re-cropping 16:9 → 9:16 by hand |
| **Motion track** | Sticky overlays without After Effects |
| **Multi-cam sync** | Aligning A/B rolls by ear |

**Hybrid approach:** Cloud ASR (Whisper / Gemini) where speech needs intelligence; **local FFmpeg** where DSP is enough — lower cost, works without every API key.

---

## SLIDE 9 — Feature deep dive: Collaboration (our wedge)

- Roles: **Owner / Editor / Viewer**  
- Email + deep-link invites; hybrid join / request-to-join  
- Viewer → Editor **role upgrade** (Zoom-style admit)  
- In-editor **Collaboration Desk**: presence, chat, activity  
- Timecode + canvas-pin comments  
- Realtime STOMP: timeline sync, live cursors/playheads, presence chimes  
- Profile photos in collab (Zoom-style faces)  
- Soft leave confirm when teammates are live  

**Industry parallel:** Figma for design · Notion for docs · **Vydora for mobile video.**

---

## SLIDE 10 — Feature deep dive: Guest review

- Create / revoke review links  
- Guests watch + leave **timestamped comments without an account**  
- Perfect for lecturers, clients, club execs  

**Problem solved:** Feedback escapes WhatsApp and lands *on the frame*.

---

## SLIDE 11 — Feature deep dive: Export, versions, ship

- Cloud export jobs: MP4 / MOV / WebM · 720p / 1080p / 4K  
- Progress stages (upload → queue → FFmpeg bake → finish)  
- Version history with **GitHub-style edit activity heatmap**  
  - Yellow day boxes = how hard the team worked that day  
  - Tap a day → who saved what and when  
  - Restore named / auto / pre-restore checkpoints  
- Project statuses including **Final Locked**  

**Problem solved:** “Which file is final?” and “Can we roll back Tuesday’s cut?”
